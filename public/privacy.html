Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

StyleCraft is a personal styling service based in Hyderabad, India. This page explains what we collect when you join our waitlist, why, and what you can ask us to do.

What we collect

  • Your email address and name, which you give us.
  • Optional details, if you choose to add them: what you are most interested in and your city.
  • A record of your consent: that you ticked the box accepting these terms and this policy, agreeing to receive emails and confirming you are 18 or older, when, and which wording you saw.
  • Where you came from (for example Instagram), taken from the link you used.

Why we collect it

  • To send you a thank-you email and to tell you when StyleCraft opens. We only email you about StyleCraft.
  • To understand which posts and pages bring people to us, using where you came from (the link you used).

We rely on your consent. You give it by ticking the box next to the sign-up form.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase stores your details in its database, and Google (Gmail) sends our emails. Our website host, Vercel, serves this page and may keep standard server logs.

We do not sell your details and we do not share them with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it. You can ask at any time, and we will remove your details.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe by replying to any email from us, or by messaging us on Instagram.
  • Ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it. We aim to answer within 7 days.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew.

To do any of these, message us on Instagram @thestyle.craft.

Age

The waitlist is for people aged 18 and over. If you are younger, please do not join.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where needed, tell you by email.
